# Stock-Market-Analysis-of-Microsoft

# Microsoft Stock Market Analysis Dashboard (1986–2022)
**Tools & Technologies:** Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power BI
**Data Source:** Kaggle
**Project Domain:** Data Analytics | Financial Data Visualization | Business Intelligence

## 1. Business Problem

Microsoft's historical stock market data from 1986 to 2022 contains valuable information about stock prices, trading volume, and daily market movements. However, analyzing this data manually makes it difficult to identify long-term price trends, compare quarterly trading activity, and understand the distribution of positive and negative trading days.

The business problem was to transform raw historical stock market data into an interactive dashboard that provides a clear overview of Microsoft's stock performance and trading patterns over time.

## 2. Goal of the Dashboard

The primary goal of this project was to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Microsoft's historical stock market performance from 1986 to 2022.

The dataset was sourced from Kaggle, processed using Microsoft Excel and Power Query, and visualized using Power BI.

The dashboard aims to:
- Monitor key stock market indicators, including average closing price, maximum high price, minimum low price, and the percentage of green trading days.
- Analyze yearly trends in average opening and closing prices.
- Compare trading volume across different quarters and years.
- Examine trading volume by day of the week.
- Compare green days (positive price movements) and red days (negative price movements).
- Enable users to explore historical data through interactive year and quarter filters.

## 3. Walkthrough of Key Visuals

The dashboard contains key visualizations, KPI cards, and interactive filters to provide a comprehensive overview of Microsoft's historical stock market performance.

**A. KPI Cards**
- Average Closing Price: Displays the average closing stock price across the selected data.
- Maximum High Price: Shows the highest recorded stock price in the selected dataset.
- Minimum Low Price: Highlights the lowest recorded stock price in the selected dataset.
- Green Day Percentage: Displays the percentage of positive trading days.

**B. Average Opening and Closing Prices by Year**

A clustered column chart compares average opening and closing stock prices across different years. It helps identify long-term price trends.

**C. Trading Volume by Quarter**

A doughnut chart displays the distribution of total trading volume across Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4, allowing comparisons of quarterly trading activity.

**D. Trading Volume by Year and Day Type**

A 100% stacked bar chart compares trading volume associated with green and red trading days across years.

**E. Trading Volume by Day Name**

A horizontal bar chart compares trading volume across Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday.

**F. Count of Trading Dates by Day Type**

A doughnut chart compares the number of green and red trading days, showing the distribution of positive and negative trading sessions.

**G. Trading Volume by Year**

A horizontal bar chart presents the yearly distribution of trading volume, enabling comparisons across different years.

**H. Interactive Filters and Slicers**

Quarter buttons and a year slicer allow users to filter the data dynamically and investigate specific periods.

## 4. Features / Highlights

- **Historical Stock Market Analysis:** Analyzes Microsoft's stock market performance from 1986 to 2022.
